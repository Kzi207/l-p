## Shared prompt

Create ONE production photo-frame overlay PNG, square 1024x1024. This is a flat front-facing scrapbook frame asset for a photo app, no perspective mockup. Intricate tactile realistic mixed-media collage, rich paper fibers, layered torn edges, elegant shadows. Theme: [theme below]. All artwork confined to the outer border, primarily outer 12% of image. The large central square from x=15% to85%, y=15% to85% must be COMPLETELY TRANSPARENT alpha, empty hole to insert user's photo. No white/gray fill in center, no checkerboard drawn in image, no sample photograph or people. Maintain a continuous square frame at outer image edges. Center aperture square, roughly 70% of canvas, no decoration crossing into it. Premium editorial scrapbook craftsmanship. Output actual transparency inside the photo opening.
